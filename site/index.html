DataSoniPrint

Turn scientific data into something you can feel and see.

▶ Launch the app

What it does

Scientific results are overwhelmingly visual — charts and graphs that exclude people with visual impairments and offer nothing tactile. DataSoniPrint converts 2D data into accessible outputs so that no single sense is required to experience it.

🖐️

Tactile 3D print

Export a watertight STL relief you can 3D-print and feel — with optional engraved or braille axis labels.

🧩

From data, formulas or images

Generate reliefs from data columns, math formulas, or images.

👁️

Live 3D preview

See the relief in 3D and scale it to your printer bed before exporting.

Who it helps

A blind or low-vision researcher can hold and feel the 3D print; a sighted researcher can read the chart — everyone works from the same underlying dataset.

How to use it

  1. Open the app with the Launch the app button above.
  2. Upload data (CSV/HDF5/NetCDF/GRIB/ASDF), an image, or enter a formula.
  3. Adjust the relief, height, and label options; preview in 3D.
  4. Download the STL and print it.
